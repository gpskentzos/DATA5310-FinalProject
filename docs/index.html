Our group looked at crime data from 2010 to 2024 at three scales.

  • Seattle (city level)
  • The United States (national level)
  • The World (global level)

Top visualization ideas

The charts we plan to build first.

  1. How is each type of crime trending in Seattle?Seattle

    Multi-line chart layered with a point chart. Different color for each type of violent crime and directly labeled instead of using a legend. Or all greyed out with focus type in red.

  2. What are Seattle's peer cities? Why are these cities Seattle's peers?National

    Heatmap of the similarity features (z-scores) for Seattle and its peer cities, which supports our method.

  3. Is Seattle's crime higher or lower than its peer cities?National

    Ranked dot plot of violent crime per 100,000 residents for Seattle and its peer cities, with Seattle highlighted.

  4. Is Seattle following the same trend as its peers?National

    Multi-line chart of crime rates over time, with peer cities in grey and Seattle in color.

  5. Is crime in the U.S. following the same trend as other countries?Global

    Multi-line chart of homicide rates over time, with other countries in grey and the U.S. in color.
